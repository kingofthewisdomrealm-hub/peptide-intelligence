# PEPTIDE INTELLIGENCE

Evidence engine for peptides and GLP-1s — not a catalog, not a clinic.

Popularity does not raise a score. An animal tendon study is not a human injury trial. A July 2026 advisory-committee vote is not an FDA approval and is not a license to compound. A COA is not sterility. Two peptides studied separately are not a studied stack.

## Evidence labels (used every time)

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **FACT** | Measured in humans, regulators, or the published record |
| **INFERENCE** | Reasonable reading of that record |
| **EXPERT OPINION** | Clinician or scientist judgment, not a trial |
| **ANIMAL DATA** | Preclinical only — does not prove human benefit |
| **ANECDOTE** | Forums, influencers, clinics |
| **MARKETING CLAIM** | Seller language |
| **UNKNOWN** | Not established |

## Operating rules

1. Evidence order: regulators and RCTs first; forums last.
2. Animal work stays in its own box.
3. Study cards include year, design, n, dose-as-studied, duration, outcome, harms, limits, replication, conflicts — when the record allows.
4. Study doses are **research context, not your dose**.
5. Non-peptides that live in “peptide” culture get the label **COMMUNITY PEPTIDE — TECHNICALLY NOT A PEPTIDE**.
6. Scores are not inflated for fame.

## What this project will not do

- Diagnose or write a personal peptide prescription
- Give individualized dosing for prescription or investigational peptides
- Treat grey-market supply as a pharmacy
- Hide negative trials, failed programs, or FDA scientist objections
- Invent synergy or treat research-chemical websites as a medical supply chain
