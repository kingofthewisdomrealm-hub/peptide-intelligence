# Peptide Periodic Table (working map)

Traffic light = **human-evidence + regulatory legitimacy**, not Instagram volume.

Snapshot date: 7 September 2026.

## Metabolism / incretin axis — strongest human evidence

| Compound | What it actually is | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Semaglutide | GLP-1 RA peptide | Approved (Ozempic / Wegovy / Rybelsus) | Large outcomes program; GI AEs; compounding copies are a separate risk class |
| Tirzepatide | GIP/GLP-1 dual agonist peptide | Approved (Mounjaro / Zepbound) | Highest approved-class weight-loss magnitude so far |
| Liraglutide | GLP-1 RA peptide | Approved (Victoza / Saxenda) | Older daily injection |
| Dulaglutide | GLP-1 RA peptide | Approved (Trulicity) | T2D |
| Retatrutide | GIP/GLP-1/glucagon triple agonist peptide | **Investigational** — Phase 3; Lilly plans BLA **Q1 2027**; not approved | Do not buy “reta” research vials and call them the trial drug |
| CagriSema | Cagrilintide (amylin analog) + semaglutide | Late-stage combo | Two peptides, one development program |
| Orforglipron | Oral small-molecule GLP-1 agonist | **COMMUNITY PEPTIDE — TECHNICALLY NOT A PEPTIDE** | Neighbor only |

## GH axis

| Compound | Status | Honest read |
|---|---|---|
| Somatropin (rhGH) | Approved products | Real drug, real harms, WADA S2 |
| Tesamorelin | Approved for HIV-associated abdominal fat (Egrifta) | One indication; not a general fat-loss license |
| Sermorelin | Historic approved diagnostic; commercial product discontinued | Compounded adult “optimization” is a different claim than the old label |
| CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin / GHRP-2 / GHRP-6 / Hexarelin | Investigational / unapproved | WADA S2.2.4. Human evidence thinner than clinic marketing |
| AOD-9604 / hGH 176–191 | Fragments | WADA-named. Fat-loss human evidence weak |

| Compound | Status |

## Tissue / “repair” research — highest hype-to-evidence gap

| Compound | Human evidence | Regulatory now |
|---|---|---|
| BPC-157 | Very limited human data; vast rodent GI/tendon literature | Not approved. PCAC recommended 503A listing; **not listed yet**. WADA **S0** |
| TB-500 / thymosin-β4 fragment | Essentially no adequate human RCTs for sports injuries | Same compounding gap. WADA **S2.3 by name** |
| KPV | Preclinical / early | PCAC-recommended, not listed |
| GHK-Cu | Some topical/cosmetic literature; injectable claims are another layer | Often sold as cosmetic or research chemical |

## Immune / thymic / neuro

| Compound | Honest bucket |
|---|---|
| Thymosin alpha-1 | Used as a drug in some countries; **not** a current U.S. FDA-approved finished product for the wellness indications people cite |
| Semax / Selank | Russian development history; Semax PCAC-recommended, not listed; human evidence outside that ecosystem is thin |
| Epitalon | Longevity folklore + pineal-peptide theory; PCAC-recommended for an insomnia framing; evidence remains weak |
| DSIP / Emideltide | PCAC **rejected** for 503A |

## Access and product-quality red zone

Anything sold as sterile injectable “for research only,” unlicensed imports, or stacked “Wolverine” kits without a lawful pharmacy + prescription pathway. Evidence score can be high for a molecule and the **product in the vial** can still be an F.
